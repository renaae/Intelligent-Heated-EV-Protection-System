# Intelligent Heated EV Protection System

## Project Overview





The Intelligent Heated EV Protection System is an AI-enabled vehicle protection concept designed to protect electric vehicles from snow, ice, and extreme cold while minimizing energy consumption.

The system combines embedded heating technology, environmental sensors, battery-temperature monitoring, weather forecasting, AI-based controls, charging integration, and a mobile application to intelligently manage winter vehicle protection.

The project applies systems engineering, AI/ML, technical program management, safety, requirements management, and verification and validation principles to the development of an intelligent EV thermal-protection concept.

## Problem Statement
Cold weather can negatively affect electric vehicle performance, including battery efficiency, available driving range, charging performance, and vehicle readiness. Snow and ice accumulation can also require manual removal before the vehicle can be safely operated.

Traditional vehicle covers provide physical protection but generally do not actively respond to changing environmental conditions. Continuous heating, on the other hand, can consume unnecessary energy.

The engineering challenge is to develop an intelligent system capable of determining when thermal protection is required, how much heating is necessary, and when heating should be reduced or stopped.
## Proposed Solution
The proposed system uses sensors, vehicle data, weather information, and AI-based decision logic to dynamically control heating zones embedded within an EV protection cover.

Potential system inputs include:

- Ambient temperature
- Surface temperature
- Battery temperature
- Snow and ice conditions
- Humidity and moisture
- Weather forecasts
- Vehicle charging status
- Available energy

The AI control system would analyze these inputs and determine the appropriate heating strategy while balancing vehicle protection, battery readiness, safety, and energy consumption.
